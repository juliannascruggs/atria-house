Restyle this room in the "Atria House" palette. Keep the architecture, windows, doors, floor plan and camera angle exactly as in the photo. Do not move walls or change room size.
Palette: bone (#EFE4D3), mushroom (#B09C8A), terracotta (#C8603A), orange ochre (#E88A3C), dark cocoa (#4B332B), bright red (#B0201B), green (#4F7A45), blue (#3F6E94), teal tile (#3F7F78).
Bone is the base for walls and curtains. Use two or three of the other colors in each room.
Furniture: a curved sectional, a rounded coffee table, a red sofa on a blue rug, dark cocoa wood, geometric art in the palette colors.
Influences: Havan (dark wood, rough stone, collected objects), Ibiza (plaster walls, terracotta, built-in seating), Nosara (warm wood, stone, open to greenery).
Mood: a clean shell with bold color in a few deliberate places. Not beige, not boho, not farmhouse.
