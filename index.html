Design proposal

Atria House

Warm and earthy, with real color: a clean shell with bold moments, so it never reads bland or beige.

Where it comes from

Three influences

Nosara

Warm wood against stone, rooms open to the green, and fewer, better-made pieces.

Ibiza

Hand-finished plaster walls, terracotta, built-in seating, and shaded outdoor rooms you actually use.

Havan

Starielle’s Hill Country lodge: dark wood, rough stone, and rich color in the rugs, art and a few special pieces.
