Design proposal

Atria House

Warm and earthy, with real color: a clean shell with bold moments, so it never reads bland or beige.

Where it comes from

Three influences

Nosara

Warm wood against stone, rooms open to the green, and fewer, better-made pieces.

Ibiza

Hand-finished plaster walls, terracotta, built-in seating, and shaded outdoor rooms you actually use.

Havan

Starielle’s Hill Country lodge: dark wood, rough stone, and rich color in the rugs, art and a few special pieces.

Palette: bone (#EFE4D3), mushroom (#B09C8A), terracotta (#C8603A), ochre (#BF8B36), dark cocoa (#4B332B), bright red (#B0201B), green (#4F7A45), blue (#8699A5), teal tile (#53726C).
Bone is the base for walls and curtains. Use two or three of the other colors in each room.
Furniture: a curved sectional, a rounded coffee table, a red sofa on a blue rug, dark cocoa wood, geometric art in the palette colors.
Influences: Nosara (warm wood, stone, open to greenery), Ibiza (plaster walls, terracotta, built-in seating), Havan (dark wood, rough stone, collected objects).
Walls throughout: pale gray-white walls with picture-frame wainscoting, tall rectangular raised panels with a slim inner molding, white baseboards, and layered white crown molding; matching paneled cased openings between rooms.
Kitchen, living and dining: pale gray-white walls with crown molding and raised rectangular wall panels; a carved ivory fireplace with brass andirons and a big dark landscape painting above it; sculptural brass and ivory chandeliers; a veined gray stone island on a honey wood base with dark wood stools and a trumpet-shade brass chandelier; low wood-framed lounge chairs with cream cushions, a charcoal sofa and a rust shell chair; handmade ceramics, branches in vases and art books.
Kitchen wall: wood paneling on the wall to the left of the stairs, in the same wood and style as the kitchen cabinets.
Office / cuddle room: a library feel, with dark built-in bookshelves and a ladder, navy paneled walls, a low marble coffee table, green and cream lounge chairs, art books and collected objects on display, and a bold statement portrait. A low, deep, light blue couch in soft matte fabric, with rounded corners and channel-stitched seat cushions. In the attached bath or powder room: a wrap-around mountain mural in plum, ochre and gold, dark navy crown molding, a chunky pewter mirror and antique brass fixtures.
Nick's bedroom and bathroom: moody slate blue and navy with charcoal, white trim and warm brass; dark wood floors; veined white marble with charcoal; a velvety charcoal armchair; a large abstract painting in blues and black; one bold oversized portrait as a statement piece.
Crown molding: in the main living area, the primary bedroom and the office cuddle room.
Primary bedroom walls: painted blue, a little lighter than a deep classic blue.
Curtains: luxe floor-to-ceiling drapes hung behind a valance, with crown molding in front of the valance.
Mood: a clean shell with bold color in a few deliberate places. Not beige, not boho, not farmhouse.
